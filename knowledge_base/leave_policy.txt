SAMPLE LEAVE POLICY - INDIA
1. This is an illustrative company policy; applicable state law and the employee handbook take precedence.
2. Example annual allowance for a full-time employee: 15 earned/privilege leave days.
3. Example annual allowance: 12 casual leave days and 12 sick leave days.
4. Allowances may be prorated for employees joining or leaving during the year.
5. Earned leave accrues monthly; HR should confirm balances and carry-forward limits.
6. Casual leave is intended for short personal needs and is normally not carried forward.
7. Notify your manager promptly when taking sick leave; provide a medical certificate if policy requires it.
8. Apply for planned leave in the HR portal, selecting dates and the leave category.
9. Request planned leave at least five working days in advance where possible.
10. Wait for manager approval before treating planned leave as confirmed.
11. For an emergency, contact your manager as soon as possible and submit the HR portal request afterward.
12. Coordinate work handover before taking an extended leave.
13. Public holidays follow the location-specific company calendar and are separate from these example balances.
14. Maternity, paternity, adoption, and other statutory leave follow applicable law and company policy.
15. Leave encashment, carry-forward, and balances are governed by applicable law and the official policy.
