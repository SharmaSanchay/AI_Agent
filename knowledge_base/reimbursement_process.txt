SAMPLE EMPLOYEE REIMBURSEMENT PROCESS - INDIA
1. Check the current travel and expense policy before spending; limits vary by role and location.
2. Get written manager approval in advance for travel, equipment, training, or unusual expenses.
3. Use the company booking method for flights and hotels when one is provided.
4. Pay only for reasonable, necessary business expenses; personal costs are not reimbursable.
5. Keep itemized bills or tax invoices showing the supplier, date, items, and amount.
6. For travel, retain approved itineraries and receipts for transport, lodging, and other eligible costs.
7. For card or UPI payments, include the itemized bill as well as proof of payment when requested.
8. Submit the claim in the company's expense portal within 30 days of the expense or trip ending.
9. Select the correct expense category and enter the business purpose, date, amount, and cost centre.
10. Attach readable receipts and the approval record; explain any missing receipt in the claim.
11. Submit expenses in INR; disclose the original currency and exchange-rate evidence for foreign costs.
12. The manager checks business purpose and approval; Finance checks receipts and policy compliance.
13. Respond promptly if Finance returns the claim for missing information or clarification.
14. Approved claims are paid to the registered payroll or bank account, typically in the next expense cycle.
15. Never split, duplicate, or misrepresent a claim; contact Finance about exceptions before submitting.
