SAMPLE HIRING AND EMPLOYEE REFERRAL PROCESS - INDIA
1. The hiring manager defines the role, required skills, location, and approved salary range.
2. HR confirms budget and approvals before publishing the vacancy.
3. Apply through the careers page using the instructions in the job posting.
4. For an employee referral, ask the employee to submit your resume through the internal referral portal.
5. Include your name, contact details, job title or requisition ID, and an up-to-date resume.
6. The referring employee should get your consent before sharing your contact details or resume.
7. Do not send confidential information from a current or former employer.
8. HR reviews applications against the role requirements and contacts shortlisted candidates.
9. Interviews may include a recruiter screen, role-related interviews, and a practical assessment.
10. Interviewers submit job-related feedback using consistent evaluation criteria.
11. HR verifies references or other details only where applicable and with required consent.
12. The selected candidate receives a written offer after internal approvals are complete.
13. Referral rewards, if offered, depend on the current referral policy and eligibility conditions.
14. The referrer should not promise an interview, offer, or referral reward.
15. Hiring decisions are based on role fit and must comply with company policy and applicable law.
