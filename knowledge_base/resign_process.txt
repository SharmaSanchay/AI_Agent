SAMPLE RESIGNATION AND OFFBOARDING PROCESS - INDIA
1. Check your appointment letter and HR policy for the notice period that applies to your role.
2. A sample company default is 30 calendar days for most roles; some roles may require 60 or 90 days.
3. Your signed employment terms and applicable law take precedence over this example.
4. Send a dated resignation email to your manager and HR, stating your proposed last working day.
5. Ask HR to acknowledge the resignation and confirm the notice end date in writing.
6. Do not assume notice can be shortened or waived; obtain written approval from HR.
7. Any notice buyout or adjustment of leave against notice must be approved and follow policy and law.
8. Agree on a handover plan with your manager, including owners, deadlines, and current project status.
9. Return company laptop, access card, keys, and other property by the agreed date.
10. Transfer work files only through approved company systems; do not copy confidential data personally.
11. Submit outstanding expense claims and complete timesheets or leave requests before your final day.
12. HR will explain final salary, eligible leave settlement, benefits, and required exit documents.
13. Complete the exit checklist and attend an exit discussion if requested.
14. Ask HR for a service or experience letter and any tax or payroll documents you need.
15. Keep personal contact and bank details current for final payroll and post-employment communication.
